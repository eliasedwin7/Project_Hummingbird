Safety

Let go of all controls and the drone hovers in place. It also hovers if this page closes or loses connection, and it lands if the connection is gone for 15 seconds. Emergency stops the motors at once and the drone drops. Use it only if it hits something. Flips need at least 50% battery. Fly indoors or with no wind. The Tello uses its downward camera to hold position, so it needs good light and a textured floor.
